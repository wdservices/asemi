The Problem

Counterfeiters copy the packaging. They can't copy the code.

i.

Look-alike products are everywhere

Fake soap, toothpaste, and household goods circulate across markets in packaging nearly identical to the original — and there's little a manufacturer can do at the point of sale.

ii.

Customers have no way to check

Without a verification method, a buyer has to trust the label, the seller, or the price — none of which reliably tell them whether what's in their hand is genuine.

iii.

Trust erodes the brand, not just the sale

Every fake bottle sold under a real brand's name damages that brand's reputation — even though the manufacturer never made or profited from it.

How It Works

From production line to a customer's phone.

01

Register & verify

Your company signs up and submits proof of ownership for your product line. We confirm you are who you say you are before anything goes live.

02

List your product

Add your product's details and images once — name, description, category — so every code links back to genuine information.

03

Generate a batch

Tell us how many units you produced. We generate that many unique codes as QR labels, ready to print and apply.

04

Customers verify instantly

A phone camera scan — or typing the code into a browser — shows the customer your product's real information, in seconds.
