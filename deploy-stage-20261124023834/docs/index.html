Asemi User Guide

Step-by-step help for setting up products, issuing codes, checking activity, and managing your manufacturer account.

Getting Started

Step 1: Register your brand. Visit the registration page and provide your company name, operating country, industry category, and an authorized work email. Business registration documents are optional during signup.

Step 2: Account verification. After registration, your account enters a pending review state. Our compliance team reviews your details within 1–2 business days. On approval you receive 20 complimentary verification codes.

Step 3: Access your dashboard. Once approved, log in to manage your product catalogue, issue serialized code batches, download 28×32mm print stickers, and monitor scan activity.

Serialized Codes & Provenance

Every physical unit receives its own unique cryptographic code (formatted ASM-XXXX-XXXXXX) rendered on a print-ready 28×32mm sticker with QR code and text fallback. All codes remain stored permanently in your Code Bank for re-export as ZIP or print sheets.

When issuing a batch, you can record lot numbers, manufacture dates, and expiry dates alongside your product SKU for full unit-level traceability. Brands can also issue or lift instant product-wide or batch-specific recalls at any time.

Pricing: Progressive volume-based pricing applies automatically in your regional currency. Your first 20 codes are free.

Consumer Verification

Consumers verify authenticity by scanning the packaging QR code with their smartphone camera, opening our built-in browser camera scanner on /verify, or entering the printed serial code manually.

The provenance certificate displays product details, SKU, manufacturer identity, lot number, manufacture and expiry dates (with automatic expiry advisories), scan count, and urgent manufacturer recall notices if issued.

Repeated scans across distant locations automatically raise a fraud warning for consumers and flag the code in your analytics and admin fraud review console.

Products: add your catalogue

Open Products in the dashboard and select New product. Enter the product name and choose its category. Add the SKU used on your packaging; it should identify this product consistently.

The description and product photos help customers recognize the item on its verification page. Regulatory approval number, default lot number, and default manufacture/expiry dates are optional. Defaults can be overridden for an individual batch. Select Create product to save. Use the edit action on a product to update its details later.

To recall a product, use its recall action, enter a clear reason, and confirm. The warning is shown when a customer checks an affected code. Lift the recall from the same product when it is resolved.

Batches: issue product codes

Open Batches & Codes and stay on New batch. Select the product, then choose a suggested code quantity or enter your own. The price summary shows the regional rate and applies available complimentary codes automatically.

Add the lot number, manufacture date, and expiry date for the production run. Check the product and quantity in the live label preview, then select Confirm & pay with Paystack. Codes are issued after checkout is confirmed. Do not submit payment twice if the confirmation is still processing.

Open History to find a previous batch and its status. Use Codes to find issued serials and access the available code/label export actions.

Analytics: review scans

Open Analytics to review consumer scan activity, scan locations, and repeat or suspicious scans. Use these patterns to identify where customers are checking products and which codes may need investigation. A scan count represents recorded verification activity, not units sold.

Billing: check charges

Open Billing to review payment and account billing information. Each new batch displays its estimate and complimentary code deduction before checkout. The final charge is calculated by the server at checkout; rely on the Paystack amount and receipt for the completed transaction.

Profile: keep your brand current

Open Profile to review and update your company information, brand identity, and compliance documents. Keep your support contact details current so customers and the Asemi review team can identify your brand.
